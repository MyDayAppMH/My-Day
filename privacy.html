My Day: Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

My Day is a small personal app that shows one person their day ahead (calendar meetings and to-dos) and lets them log how the day went. This page explains what happens to that information.

Back to the app

What the app reads or changes

  • Google Calendar: read-only access to the events on the calendars you have switched on, including title, time, place, description and the people invited. It cannot create, change or delete events.
  • Google Health: read-only access to daily steps, calories burned, sleep and heart-rate figures, used to show how you slept and how active you were.
  • Notion: the app reads your task database and, for the people in a meeting, your contact records, so it can show who you are meeting. It can mark tasks done and add tasks you create in the app. It does nothing else in Notion.

Where information is kept

  • Your daily reviews (mood, energy, notes) and meeting prep notes are stored only in your phone's browser storage. They are never sent to a server.
  • Calendar, task and health data is fetched on request by a small server program (a Cloudflare Worker) that you or the app owner control, and passed to the app on your phone. The app keeps a recent copy on your phone so it can show it without a connection.
  • Access tokens for Google and Notion are stored in that same private Cloudflare account. The server is protected by a secret key, so only the app owner's phone can use it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not show advertising or use your information for advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools and do not build profiles of you.
  • No person reads your calendar, tasks or health data. The app only shows it back to you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Stop Google access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove access. The app can then no longer read your calendar or health data.
  • Stop Notion access: remove the integration's connections in Notion (Settings, Connections).
  • Delete your reviews: clear this site's data in your browser settings, or remove the installed app. Settings has an export option if you want a copy first.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. For questions, open an issue in the app's repository: github.com/MyDayAppMH/My-Day.
